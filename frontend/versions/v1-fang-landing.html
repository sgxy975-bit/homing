    Trust

    Evidence, not promises.

    You shouldn't have to take our word for anything. Every claim on this page can be checked.

    Non-custodial by design

    Homing never holds keys or funds. The vault only uses allowances each wallet granted, and you can revoke them at any time.

    Two independent checks

    Reactive Network decides when to ask. The vault re-checks the deadline with its own data. If it's not time, it skips and moves nothing.

    No last-minute swaps

    Changing where assets go, or who inherits, only takes effect after a waiting period you can cancel.

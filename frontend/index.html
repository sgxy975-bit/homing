How it works

Three contracts, one promise.

01

Register

Add each wallet to the registry on Sepolia and tag it daily, cold or sweepable.

02

Watch

A Reactive contract on Lasna listens to every outgoing transfer, across chains. Activity resets the clock.

03

Bring home

When a sweepable wallet stays quiet past its limit, the vault moves its tokens back to your main wallet.

Your money shouldn't get lost just because you did.
